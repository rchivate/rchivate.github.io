Yoga Research

This project focuses on researching and understanding the principles and practices of yoga.

Definitions of Yoga

Bhagavad Gita

"samatvam yoga uchyate"
yoga is a state of harmony

"yogaH karmasu kaushalam"
yoga is a skill of working: Wise person abandons judgement of good and bad and focuses on the skill in performing actions.

Types of Karma:

  • Karma - doing the action
  • Akarma - not doing the action
  • Vikarma - i dont want to do but forced to do

Yoga Sutra

"yogaH chitta vRtti nirodhaH"
yoga is mastery over mind

Yoga Vasishtha

"manaH prasamana upayaH"

Technique to calm the mind

Real Nature (normal): happy and peaceful

पञ्चकोशः (pa~nchakoshaH)

  • अन्नमयकोशः (annamayakoshaH) - the physical body
  • प्राणमयकोशः (prANamayakoshaH) - the energy body
  • मनमयकोशः (manomayakoshaH) - emotions
  • विज्ञानमयकोशः (vij~nnamayakoshaH) - intelligence
  • आनन्दमयकोशः (aanandamayakoshaH) - bliss
