Jnaana Labs

The goal of this collective project is to gather information through meticulous research about the library of knowledge that exists within the culture rooted in Sanatana Dharma. Sanatana Dharma encompasses the cultures that exist within modern-day Hinduism, Sikhism, Jainism, Buddhism, etc. The west has adopted many aspects of this culture, but not without changing them significantly. For example, what was once a spiritual, individual experience, is now a 1 billion dollar industry. Yes, yoga! The goal of this project is to detangle the core concepts of this philosophy and really understand the presented knowledge down to its core without inflicting any sort of judgement.

About the projects

All of the projects in this initiative aim to display the knowledge in its most authentic form. There is no emotional or patriotic attachment. All the knowledge used to develop these projects is available to reference. The research is properly cited to ensure facts rooted deep in science. Rather than proving Indian philosophy using western science, these projects display the depth of knowledge that was discovered centuries before and outlining the logic behind it all. All the research for these projects is done with a bottom up approach to eliminate any confirmation bias that may come up innately while reverse engineering.
